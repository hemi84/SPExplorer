// 01 — Introduction

Beyond Anomaly Mapping

Self-Potential surveys are among the most cost-effective geophysical methods for exploring sulfide-rich mineral systems. Yet traditional interpretation has long been limited to visualizing surface anomalies. SPExplorer changes that.

The Problem with Conventional SP

Traditional SP interpretation maps surface anomalies but provides no depth information, 3D geometry, or subsurface current density distribution — leaving exploration teams with ambiguous targets.

The SPExplorer Solution

SPExplorer transforms surface SP measurements into full 3D current density models, revealing the geometry, depth, and distribution of subsurface electrochemical sources driving the SP response.

Scientific Foundation

Built on rigorous geophysical inverse theory, the software jointly inverts SP data for current density source distributions within a realistic 3D topographic model, with full misfit tracking and uncertainty quantification.

// 04 — Geological Significance

What High Current Density Zones Mean

In mineral exploration, zones of elevated current density in the SP inversion model may indicate areas of concentrated electrochemical activity — often spatially associated with sulfide mineralisation and structurally controlled fluid pathways.

  • ✅ Connected sulfide accumulations generating electrolytic current loops
  • ✅ Massive or semi-massive sulfide bodies with high electronic conductivity
  • ✅ Redox boundaries favorable for metal precipitation and sulfide stability
  • ✅ Faults and fluid pathways that focused mineralizing hydrothermal fluids
  • ✅ Alteration halos and graphitic units with anomalous electrochemical contrast

From Surface to Drill Target

By converting SP measurements into 3D current density distributions, geoscientists move beyond anomaly mapping and gain a quantitative understanding of potential mineralisation controls. Depth extents, dip directions, and lateral continuity are all recoverable from the inversion model.

Low-Cost, High-Impact Prioritisation

As exploration targets become deeper and more concealed beneath cover, advanced SP interpretation offers a low-cost tool for target ranking, geochemical anomaly evaluation, and drill-hole planning ahead of expensive drilling campaigns.

Integration with Other Methods

SPExplorer model outputs — current density volumes, flow-proxy vectors, and depth sections — are exportable in standard formats for integration with IP, MT, gravity, or seismic datasets in multi-physics interpretation workflows.

Transparent Uncertainty Tracking

Every inversion result is accompanied by full misfit statistics, residual maps, and convergence diagnostics — so geoscientists can assess model reliability and make defensible decisions backed by quantitative metrics.
